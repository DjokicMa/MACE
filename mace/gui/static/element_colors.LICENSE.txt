element_colors.json holds four element color schemes:

- "vesta" and "jmol": the VESTA and Jmol colors of pymatgen's
  src/pymatgen/vis/ElementColorSchemes.yaml (commit 9100860d), under the MIT
  License below (The Materials Project / LBNL).
- "cvd" (Colorblind safe): MACE's own scheme, under MACE's MIT License
  (Copyright (c) 2021-2026 Marcus Djokic, Mendoza Group, Michigan State
  University; see LICENSE at the repository root). It is generated by
  mace/gui/tools/make_element_colors.py (cvd_scheme) from the hues of the
  Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe palette: M. Okabe and K. Ito, "Color Universal
  Design (CUD): How to make figures and presentations that are friendly to
  colorblind people" (2002, revised 2008), a published recommendation of
  eight colors that anyone may use. No color table was copied from it.
- "legacy": the colors `mace plotting` drew structures in before 1.1.6
  (Plotting/plottingCIFs.py's ELEMENT_COLORS, gray for other elements),
  MACE's own, under MACE's MIT License.

Your own palettes (View > Element Colors...) are yours; MACE keeps them in
$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/mace/element_colors.json (default ~/.config/mace/).
